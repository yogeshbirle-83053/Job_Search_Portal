About JobPortal

Empowering professionals and companies to discover the right fit through modern web technology.

Our Mission

JobPortal was built to remove friction from the hiring process. Whether you are a fresher looking for your first breakthrough opportunity or a high-growth company searching for experienced talent, our platform provides the tools, transparent status tracking, and database-backed workflows you need.

Why Choose JobPortal?

  • Verified Listings: Direct job postings from authentic employer accounts.
  • Transparent Tracking: Real-time status changes from Applied to Under Review, Shortlisted, and Hired.
  • Enterprise Security: Strict server-side role permissions, encrypted passwords, and secure file handling.
